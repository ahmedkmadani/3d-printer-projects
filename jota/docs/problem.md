# The problem

If a feature is not on this page, it is not part of Jota.
No solution here on purpose — no device, no app.

## Who

One person: me. Not a market.

## When it happens

- **After therapy** — the thoughts are clear for a few minutes, then gone.
- **A thought pops up** — walking, waiting, half asleep. It does not wait.
- **Hands busy** — driving. The thought comes, but I cannot pick up the phone.

The same thing every time: **the thought does not last long, and saving it takes too long.**

## Why the phone does not work

| | |
|---|---|
| Too slow | Unlock, find app, tap record. The thought is gone by then |
| **It is the distraction** | I open the phone, I see everything else. Thought lost, ten minutes gone. This happens before the app even opens, so a better app cannot fix it |
| Cannot use it driving | And driving is one of the three moments |
| Does not help me come back | See below |

## Coming back is the hard part

Saving is easy. Coming back is where voice memo apps fail. You get a list of
recordings with dates. You never play them. You cannot skim audio.

I need three things:

- **Readable** — text I can scan in 10 seconds
- **Sorted** — a 10 minute talk has about 3 real things in it
- **Connected** — the same worry in 4 sessions is one finding, not 4 notes

If I never come back to it, saving it was pointless.

## Privacy is about how I speak

**If I am not sure where it goes, I speak differently.** A thought I softened
while saying it is not the real thought.

Open, honest speech is the whole value. So privacy is needed first — not a
feature to trade away later for convenience.

## It works when

1. I can save a thought without picking up or looking at my phone. About 2 seconds.
2. It works while driving, walking, or in the dark.
3. Saving a thought never turns into checking my phone.
4. Later I can **read** it, not play it.
5. What I read is **sorted**.
6. Repeating topics show up on their own.
7. I never stop to think before I speak.
8. **I still use it after a month.** This is the real test.

## Not this

- Not a meeting recorder — one voice, just me
- Not a to-do app — no tasks, no checkboxes
- Not journalling — that is planned, this is not
- Not an audio library — audio is the input, text is the output
- Not for other people. Not yet.

## Open question

**What language do I speak?** This is the only thing here that changes the
build. Offline speech-to-text is much worse outside English. It may force a
choice between #7 and #4.
